# ACIR detail-page reads

Text extracted from ACIR requirement documents on 2026-08-27, by walking the
Salesforce shadow roots in a browser. Kept because
`docs/reference-layer-curation.md` §2.2 and §2.3 make claims about what these
pages contain and how they misread, and those claims should be checkable
without re-fetching a page that may have changed.

**These are extractions, not rules, and they are not even reliable text.** §2.3
exists because the prohibition document below came out with its negation
displaced — the flattened reading says permits are issued when the page says
they will not be. Read them against the rendered page, never instead of it.

Each file records the source URL and the retrieval date. Naming:
`<rowId>_<short-name>.txt`.
